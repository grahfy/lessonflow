# Initial Concept
LessonFlow is an MIT-licensed platform for individual music teachers and music schools who want to automate the admin work around lessons.

# Product Guide: LessonFlow

## Overview
LessonFlow is a comprehensive management platform tailored for music schools and individual teachers to automate administrative workflows, including bookings, invoicing, reporting, and student resources.

## Target Audience
- **Solo Music Teachers:** Looking to reduce time spent on scheduling and billing.
- **Music Schools:** Small to medium-sized institutions needing a centralized system for multiple teachers and students.
- **Admin Staff:** Supporting appointment and billing workflows in a music education context.

## Core Pillars
- **Scheduling:** Public booking requests, admin calendar management, and recurring lesson series.
- **Invoicing:** Full financial lifecycle from draft to payment, including PDF generation and automated reminders.
- **Student Portal:** Authenticated access for students to view their schedule and learning materials (audio/PDF).
- **Reporting:** Operational and revenue dashboards for school owners.

## Key Features
- **Appointment Management:** Approve, reject, cancel, or move appointments with ease.
- **Recurring Series:** Manage long-term lesson commitments automatically.
- **Financial Integrity:** Invoices denormalize customer data at creation for historical accuracy.
- **Unified Communication History:** Integrated Gmail support with two-way synchronization for a complete view of student interactions.
- **Whitelabeling:** Easily rebrand the platform via environment variables and an admin settings panel.
- **Resource Management:** Assign audio and PDF materials to specific students or appointments.

## Strategic Goals
- **Efficiency:** Minimize the time teachers spend on non-teaching tasks.
- **Professionalism:** Provide students and parents with a polished, automated experience.
- **Visibility:** Give school owners clear insights into revenue and operational performance.
